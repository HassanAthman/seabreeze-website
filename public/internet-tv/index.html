What you need

A steady internet connection

We recommend at least 10 Mbps for smooth HD viewing on one screen. Streaming uses data, so an unlimited home plan is best. Airtel 5G home internet (15 or 30 Mbps, 1,000 GB fair use) works well, and we can install it for you.

Common questions

Do I need Airtel 5G to watch?

No. Internet TV works on any reliable home internet. If you need a connection, we can install Airtel 5G home internet for you.

Which channels are included?

The line-up is set by the service provider and depends on its licensing, so it can change. Ask us on WhatsApp for the current list in your languages before you pay.

How much does it cost?

It is an annual subscription. Message us on WhatsApp 0734 782 203 for the current price.

Can I watch on more than one device?

It depends on the plan. Ask us how many screens are included before you subscribe.

Can I show it in a bar, hotel or restaurant?

No. The service is for private viewing at home. Showing it to the public or charging people to watch needs separate licences.
