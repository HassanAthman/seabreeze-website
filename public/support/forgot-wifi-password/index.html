I forgot my WiFi password

You can find it or set a new one.

Takes about 1 minute

  1. 1

    Look at the label on the router

    If you never changed the password, it is printed there.

  2. 2

    Changed it before? Set a new one

    Use a phone that is still connected and follow Change your WiFi name and password.

  3. 3

    No device can connect? Call us

    Do not reset the router before talking to us.
