Get to know your 5G router

Drag to turn the router. Tap a number to see what each part does, or play the restart guide.

An example 5G router. Yours may look a little different.

Tap a number

Each number on the router shows one part. Start with 1, the lights on the front.

1. Status lights

Power, signal and WiFi lights. When all three are on, your internet is working. If the signal light is off or blinking for long, restart the router or move it nearer a window.

2. Label

Shows the default WiFi name and password and the router's serial number. If you never changed your WiFi password, use the one on this label.

3. Power socket

Use only the charger that came with the router. To restart, unplug here, wait 30 seconds, and plug back in.

4. Network (LAN) ports

Connect a TV, computer or CCTV recorder with a network cable for the steadiest connection.

5. Reset hole

Do not press this unless we ask you to. A reset deletes your WiFi name and password and all settings. Call or WhatsApp us first.

  1. Unplug the power cable from the back of the router.
  2. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug the power cable back in.
  4. Wait 2 to 3 minutes until the power, signal and WiFi lights are on. Then connect again.

  1. Connect your phone or laptop to the router's WiFi.
  2. Open any browser, for example Chrome.
  3. Type 192.168.1.1 in the address bar and open it.
  4. Tap Login at the top right.
  5. Enter admin as the username and admin as the password.
  6. Open WiFi settings.
  7. Choose 5G WiFi settings.
  8. Switch Optimize ON.
  9. Type your new WiFi name and password.
  10. Tap SAVE to finish.

After saving: your phones and TVs disconnect. Connect them again with the new name and password. Use a password of at least 8 characters that you can remember. The screens above are examples, so your router's pages may look a little different.

I forgot my WiFi password

If any phone or laptop is still connected, follow the steps in How to change your WiFi name and password to see or set a new password.

If you never changed it, the password is on the label on the router (number 2 on the 3D router above).

If no device can connect, WhatsApp us on 0734 782 203. Do not reset the router before talking to us.

How to restart your router
  1. Unplug the power cable from the back of the router.
  2. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug the power cable back in.
  4. Wait 2 to 3 minutes until the power, signal and WiFi lights are on. Then connect again.

Watch the restart guide in 3D

Where to put your router for the best signal
  • Near a window, on a shelf or table, not on the floor.
  • Away from metal roofs, thick walls, fridges and microwaves.
  • In the middle of the house if you can, so every room gets WiFi.
  • Keep it switched on. Turning it off and on many times a day slows it down.

Pay and renew guides

How to renew with M-Pesa (Paybill 4129691)
  1. Open M-Pesa and choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill.
  2. Business number: 4129691.
  3. Account number: your Airtel 5G line number, for example 0101234567.
  4. Amount: your package price, for example KES 1,999 or KES 2,999.
  5. Enter your M-Pesa PIN. You get an SMS when your line is renewed.

Any phone with M-Pesa works, including a kabambe. See the full renewal guide

Pay in small amounts (Lipa Mdogo Mdogo)

Send any amount from KES 500 to Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G number as the account. Payments add up, and when they reach your package price we renew your line. There is no extra charge. How Lipa Mdogo Mdogo works

Can I pay before my plan expires?

Yes. Pay any time. Your new plan starts when the current one ends, so you never lose days.

Paying for a family member or a different line

Use their Airtel 5G line number as the account number. You can pay from any M-Pesa line.

I used the wrong account number

WhatsApp us on 0734 782 203 with the M-Pesa message and the correct Airtel 5G number. We move the payment to the right line.

Fix a problem

I paid but my internet is still off
  1. Check that you got the M-Pesa message and the business number was 4129691.
  2. Wait 30 minutes. Most renewals reflect within minutes.
  3. Restart the router.
  4. Still off? WhatsApp the M-Pesa message and your Airtel 5G number to 0734 782 203.
No internet and I have not paid

Your plan may have ended. Check your expiry date, then renew on Paybill 4129691. Short of the full amount? Use Lipa Mdogo Mdogo.

My internet is slow
  • Restart the router.
  • Move it nearer a window or higher up.
  • Too many phones and TVs streaming at once share the speed. Try 30 Mbps if your home uses a lot.
  • After 1,000 GB in one plan, the speed drops to 2 Mbps until you renew.
The router lights are off

Check the power cable and socket, or try another socket. If the power light is still off, WhatsApp us on 0734 782 203. Do not open the router.
