Renew with M-Pesa

Pay for your Airtel 5G from any phone using Paybill 4129691.

Takes about 2 minutes

  1. 1

    Open M-Pesa

    Choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill.

    Safaricom12:40
    Lipa na M-PESA
    1 Pay Bill
    2 Buy Goods
    OKBack
  2. 2

    Enter the business number

    Type 4129691.

    Safaricom12:40
    Business no.
    4129691
    OKBack
  3. 3

    Enter your Airtel 5G number as the account

    For example 0101234567.

    Safaricom12:40
    Account no.
    0101234567
    OKBack
  4. 4

    Enter the amount

    KES 1,999 for 15 Mbps or KES 2,999 for 30 Mbps.

    Safaricom12:40
    Amount
    1999
    OKBack
  5. 5

    Enter your PIN and send

    You get an SMS when your line is renewed.

    Acc 0101234567

    OKBack
