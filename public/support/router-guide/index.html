Get to know your router

Turn the 3D router with your finger and tap a number to see what each part does.

Takes about 2 minutes

An example 5G router. Yours may look a little different.

Tap a number

Each number on the router shows one part. Start with 1, the lights on the front.

1. Status lights

Power, signal and WiFi. When all three are on, your internet is working.

2. Label

Shows the default WiFi name and password. If you never changed your password, use this one.

3. Power socket

Use only the charger that came with the router. To restart, unplug here for 30 seconds.

4. Network (LAN) ports

Connect a TV, computer or CCTV recorder with a cable for the steadiest connection.

5. Reset hole

Do not press it. A reset deletes your WiFi name, password and settings. Call us first.

  1. Unplug the power cable from the back of the router.
  2. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug the power cable back in.
  4. Wait 2 to 3 minutes until the power, signal and WiFi lights are on. Then connect again.
