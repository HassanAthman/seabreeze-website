My internet is slow

Try these steps in order. Most slow connections are fixed by the first two.

Takes about 2 minutes

  1. 1

    Restart the router

    Unplug it, wait 30 seconds, then plug it back in. See how

  2. 2

    Move the router near a window

    Put it high up, away from metal and thick walls.

  3. 3

    Check how many devices are on

    Many phones and TVs streaming at once share the speed.

  4. 4

    Check your data use

    After 1,000 GB in one plan, the speed drops to 2 Mbps until you renew.
