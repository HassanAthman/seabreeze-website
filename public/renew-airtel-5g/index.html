    Renewal questions

    What is the Airtel 5G paybill number?

    Seabreeze's paybill for Airtel 5G Smart Connect renewals is 4129691. Use your Airtel 5G line number as the account number.

    Can I pay for Airtel 5G without the MyAirtel app?

    Yes. Use M-Pesa on any phone: Lipa na M-Pesa, Pay Bill, 4129691, your 5G number, the amount, your PIN.

    Can I renew before my plan expires?

    Yes. The new plan starts as soon as the current one ends, so you never lose days.
