Questions

What is Lipa Mdogo Mdogo?

A way to pay for your Airtel 5G home internet in small amounts. Send any amount from KES 500 to M-Pesa Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G line number as the account. Seabreeze adds up your payments and renews your line when the total reaches your package price.

How much do I need to reach?

KES 1,999 for 15 Mbps or KES 2,999 for 30 Mbps, for 30 days.

When does my line renew?

If your line is still active, the new 30 days start when the current plan ends, so you lose no days. If it has already gone off, it renews the day your total is complete.

How do I know my balance?

After every payment we send you an SMS with the amount received and the balance left.

Is there a minimum amount or an extra charge?

The minimum payment is KES 500. Seabreeze adds no extra charge: you pay only the package price.

What if I pay more than the price?

The extra stays as credit for your line and counts toward your next month.
