About Seabreeze

Connecting Kenyan homes, from Malindi

Seabreeze Data Ventures Ltd (Sea Breeze) is an authorised Airtel Kenya Enterprise Solution Partner. We connect homes and businesses to Airtel 5G and help every customer stay online with simple renewals on M-Pesa Paybill 4129691.

Get Airtel 5G Visit the help centre

One outdoor 5G unit. Fast WiFi for the whole home.

Who we are

A local partner for Airtel 5G

We are based in Malindi on the Kenyan coast, with agents across the Coast, Nairobi, the Rift Valley and western Kenya. Our agents meet you at home, check the signal, send your request to Airtel and stay in touch after installation.

Many customers struggle to renew when they have no smartphone or the MyAirtel app. So we made renewal work from any phone: pay Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G number, and we renew your line. You can even pay in small amounts with Lipa Mdogo Mdogo.

We are also an Airtel Money super agent, serving float to Airtel Money agents.
