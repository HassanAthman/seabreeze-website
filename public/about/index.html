About Seabreeze

Connecting Kenyan homes, from Malindi

Seabreeze Data Ventures Ltd (Sea Breeze) is a Malindi company for computer networking, structured cabling, CCTV and IPTV. We are also an authorised Airtel Kenya Enterprise Solution Partner: we connect homes and businesses to Airtel 5G and help every customer stay online with simple renewals on M-Pesa Paybill 4129691.

Who we are

An authorised Airtel partner, close to you

We are based in Malindi on the Kenyan coast, with agents across the Coast, Nairobi, the Rift Valley and western Kenya. Our agents meet you at home, check the signal, send your request to Airtel and stay in touch after installation.

Many customers struggle to renew when they have no smartphone or the MyAirtel app. So we made renewal work from any phone: pay Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G number, and we renew your line. You can even pay in small amounts with Lipa Mdogo Mdogo.

Our sister company Outspan Communication, a separate registered business in the same office, is an official Airtel franchise partner (home broadband division). It handles Airtel SIM cards and Airtel Money, and serves float to Airtel Money agents as a super agent.
