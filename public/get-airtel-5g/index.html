How it works

Six steps from this form to WiFi

  1. Send your details

    Your first and last name as on your ID, a phone number you can receive SMS on, your email and where you want the router. It takes two minutes.

  2. Confirm your number with a code

    We register you with Airtel. Airtel sends a one-time code (OTP) by SMS to your phone. Our team contacts you from 0782 998 842 or 0734 782 203 to ask for it. That gives you an Airtel sales lead number.

    Only share the code with Seabreeze on 0782 998 842 or 0734 782 203. Never share it with anyone else.

  3. Pay with the Airtel payment link

    Airtel sends a payment request to your phone. You pay Airtel directly: your package price, plus KES 1,000 connection fee on 30-day plans.

    Each link works once. If your payment does not go through, tell us and we send a new one. Or pay through Paybill 4129691 and we complete the Airtel payment for you (see below).

  4. Airtel assigns your installer

    Once payment is confirmed, Airtel sends you the installer's name and phone number. The installer calls to agree a time to visit.

  5. Installation and ID check

    The installer checks the signal at your place, registers the router SIM with your original ID or passport, installs the outdoor unit, sets up WiFi and tests the speed with you. You sign a job completion certificate.

    You never pay the installer cash or anything extra.

  6. Renew every month from any phone

    Pay on M-Pesa Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G line number as the account. We send you an SMS reminder before your plan ends.

Have these ready: your original national ID or passport, the phone that will get the code, and the payment amount.
