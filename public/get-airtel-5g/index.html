How it works

Six steps from this form to WiFi

  1. Send your details

    Your first and last name as on your ID, your phone number and where you want the router. No email needed. It takes two minutes.

  2. We confirm your details

    Our team calls you from 0782 998 842 or 0734 782 203 to confirm your name, location and package.

  3. Pay on Paybill 4129691

    Pay KES 2,999 for 15 Mbps or KES 3,999 for 30 Mbps, with your name as the account. That is your first 30 days plus the KES 1,000 connection fee.

    We pay Airtel for you from our Airtel Money account. No Airtel payment prompt that can fail, no email or smartphone needed, and no extra charge to send money to Airtel.

  4. Airtel assigns your installer

    Once we have paid, Airtel assigns an installer, who calls you. Give the installer directions to your place.

  5. Installation and ID check

    The installer registers the router SIM with your original ID or passport and connects the router. Airtel then sends you a Happy code by SMS: share it with the installer. The installer sets your WiFi name and password, and you are online.

    You never pay the installer cash or anything extra.

  6. We ask for your 5G WiFi number

    About a day after you pay, we SMS you to ask for your new 5G WiFi number, so your renewal reminders start. Renew every month on M-Pesa Paybill 4129691 with that number as the account.

Have these ready: your original national ID or passport, KES 2,999 or 3,999 on M-Pesa, and directions to your place. Often done the same day if you start early.
Good to know

What new clients ask us

KES 2,999 to start

On 15 Mbps: your first month (KES 1,999) plus the KES 1,000 connection fee. On 30 Mbps it is KES 3,999. Pay on Paybill 4129691 with your name as the account.

A portable router with a power bank

The router works with a SIM card, so you can move it around your home. It comes with a power bank for when the power goes off.

Why an installer visits

The installer checks the signal where you will use the router, so you get good service. That is why you do not take a router home from our office.

How soon can I connect?

Often the same day if you start early, but we cannot promise it. Our office and Airtel installers work until 5pm.

Receipts

Your payment message is your receipt. Claiming from your employer? Ask us for a Seabreeze receipt with our company name and we send it on WhatsApp. It is not an ETR tax receipt.

Visit our office

Opposite Co-operative Bank, Malindi, open until 5pm. You do not need to visit: everything works by phone.
