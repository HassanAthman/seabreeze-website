    Questions

    Common questions

    Do I need a smartphone or the MyAirtel app?

    No. Any phone with M-Pesa works. Go to Lipa na M-Pesa, Pay Bill, enter 4129691 and your Airtel 5G line number as the account number.

    Can I pay for Airtel 5G in small amounts?

    Yes, with Lipa Mdogo Mdogo. Send any amount from KES 500 to Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G number as the account. Payments add up, and when they reach KES 1,999 (15 Mbps) or KES 2,999 (30 Mbps) we renew your line. You get an SMS with your balance after every payment. There is no extra charge from Seabreeze. More

    How do I check my expiry date without *400#?

    WhatsApp or SMS us your Airtel 5G number and we reply with your expiry date and the amount to pay.

    Can I renew before my plan expires?

    Yes. Pay any time through Paybill 4129691. Your new plan starts as soon as the current one ends, so you never lose days.

    I paid but my internet is still off.

    Wait 30 minutes, then restart the router. Still off? Send us the M-Pesa confirmation message and we follow up.

    My 5G was set up by someone else. Can I still pay through Seabreeze?

    Yes. Any Airtel 5G home customer can renew through Paybill 4129691.

    Is Seabreeze part of Airtel?

    Seabreeze Data Ventures Ltd is an authorised Airtel Kenya Enterprise Solution Partner (ESP). We connect new Airtel 5G home customers and help existing ones renew.

    Does it work if my area has no 5G?

    Yes. The Smart Connect router works in both 5G and 4G areas.

    Is the data really unlimited?

    Yes, with a fair use limit of 1,000 GB per plan. After that you keep browsing at 2 Mbps until you renew, or you can buy a 500 GB booster for KES 1,000.

    How do I stop promotional SMS?

    Use our SMS preferences form to stop offers, renewal reminders or both, or text STOP to 0782 998 842. Payment receipts are always sent.
