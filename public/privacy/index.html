Privacy and SMS policy

Your details are safe with us

This page explains what Seabreeze Data Ventures Ltd keeps about you, why, who sees it, and how to stop our SMS. Last updated 25 November 2026.

We will never ask for

Your M-Pesa PIN, Airtel Money PIN, or any code sent to your phone. We never ask you to send money to a personal number. Pay only to Paybill 4129691 with your Airtel 5G line as the account.

Who we are

Seabreeze Data Ventures Ltd (Sea Breeze), Malindi, Kilifi County. An authorised Airtel Kenya Enterprise Solution Partner (ESP). We are a partner, not Airtel Kenya itself.

What we keep

Your name, the phone number you want SMS on, your Airtel 5G line (router) number, your plan and expiry date, and the paybill payments made for your line. If you fill a form on this website, we keep what you typed.

Why we keep it

To renew your 5G on time, send renewal reminders and payment confirmations, help you when something is wrong, and tell you about offers on your own 5G service.

Who sees it

Only our staff and our authorised agents. We share what is needed with Airtel Kenya to connect and renew your line, and with our SMS provider to deliver messages. We do not sell your details to anyone.

How long

While you are our client, and up to 7 years after for payment records the law asks us to keep. Ask us and we will delete anything we do not need to keep.

SMS

Our SMS and how to stop them

What we send

Payment confirmations, renewal reminders before your 5G expires, and now and then an offer for your 5G. Messages come from our sender name and always point to Paybill 4129691 and this website.

Stop offers

Text STOP to 0782 998 842, or use the SMS page. We stop offers within 24 hours. You will still get confirmations for payments you make.

Fake messages

If an SMS uses our name but asks for a PIN, a different paybill, or money to a personal number, it is not from us. Call 0782 998 842 or WhatsApp 0734 782 203 to check.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to marketing. Email support@seabreezedata.co.ke and we reply within 7 days.

Still need help?

Talk to a person. We answer from 8am to 8pm, every day.
