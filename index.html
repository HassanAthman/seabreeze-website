Frequently asked questions

Can I renew before my subscription expires?

According to the supplied Airtel flyer, early renewal and plan changes may be possible, with the next plan taking effect after the current validity period. Confirm current terms with Airtel.

Can you confirm coverage in my area?

Send your town or neighbourhood using the enquiry form. Our team will help check availability; service coverage must be confirmed.

How do I change my router Wi-Fi password?

Router menus vary by model. Contact support for guidance. Never send your router administrator password or M-Pesa PIN through a form or WhatsApp.

How can I stop promotional messages?

Text STOP to 0782998842 or call customer support and request removal from promotional messaging.
